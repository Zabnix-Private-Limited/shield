# Customer App Deferred Scope

| Capability | Why deferred / missing contract | User impact | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary phone change | Firebase re-verification, identity conflict and session/audit policy | Contact support required | P1 product/backend design |
| Account deletion / export | Medical, financial and retention/privacy policy absent | Not exposed | P1 legal/product design |
| Wallet top-up, statement export, redemption expiry | customer payment/redemption/export contracts absent | Read-only wallet/rewards | P1 backend/product |
| Cart, checkout, payment, delivery | catalogue-only release decision | Wellness cannot be purchased in app | P1 product/backend |
| Provider ratings, favourites, exact distance | supporting data and safety policy absent | Discovery remains factual/basic | P2 |
| Real slots, pricing, coverage, dependent booking, online visit | appointment/patient/coverage contract absent | Booking is a request only | P1 backend/product |
| Secure sharing, OCR/clinical interpretation, fulfilment | privacy/clinical workflow and provider contracts absent | Documents/prescriptions remain archival/request-only | P1 backend/product |
| Order tracking/cancel/refund | customer commerce fulfilment contract absent | Recorded orders are read-only | P1 backend/product |
| Referral native share/QR and notification deep-link pagination | dedicated safe contract absent | Existing referral/inbox works without expansion | P2 |
