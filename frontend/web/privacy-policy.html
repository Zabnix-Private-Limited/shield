Welcome to SHIELD. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how SHIELD collects, uses, stores, protects, and shares your information when you use the SHIELD mobile application and related web services.

SHIELD is a healthcare and wellness platform developed to help users access healthcare-related services, manage digital health records, track benefits, use wallet services, and interact with authorized healthcare providers and partners.

Depending on how you use SHIELD, we may collect the following information:

SHIELD uses mobile number verification through One-Time Password (OTP) authentication to verify user identity.

  • Manage wallet balances and transactions.
